Metal box sound slices
Source: Aurelon - Unlocking a metal box
https://freesound.org/people/Aurelon/sounds/698200/
License: CC0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
19 isolated sound groups, numbered in recording order. Includes short clicks and longer handling/rattle groups.
Edits: silence trimming, up to +12dB gain with 0.84 peak ceiling, 3ms edge fades, mono 44.1kHz 16-bit WAV. No synthetic layers.
See SliceIndex.csv for exact source timings. Existing game cues have not been overwritten by these slices.
